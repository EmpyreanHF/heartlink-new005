Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: November 14, 2026

App: Heart Link · Developer: Empyrean App (Akhigbe Allen)

You can delete your Heart Link account and data in two ways.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open Heart Link and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile tab and tap the Settings icon at the top right.
  3. Under Safety & legal, tap Delete my data, enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email heartlink43@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Heart Link account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days of your request.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, videos and voice notes.
  • Your matches, chats and live rooms you hosted.
  • Your swipes, blocks and sign-in account.

What may be kept

Deleting the account in the app removes your profile, photos, matches and chats straight away. Limited records may be kept for up to 12 months, or longer where the law requires it (for example purchase records), or to stop a banned person from returning. Unused tokens are lost when the account is deleted and cannot be refunded by us.

See our Privacy Policy for more.
