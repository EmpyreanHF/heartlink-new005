Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Heart Link is for adults aged 18 and over. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Child safety contact: heartlink43@gmail.com
Use this address to report a child-safety concern or to contact the person responsible for child safety at Heart Link.

Our standards

  • Adults only. Members must confirm they are 18 or older when they sign up. Accounts we believe belong to someone under 18 are removed.
  • Prohibited content and behavior. Sexual content involving minors, grooming, sexualizing minors, or any attempt to contact or exploit a minor is banned. See our Terms.
  • Live rooms. Live video from broadcasters is checked automatically for nudity and sexual content. Private rooms are only for adults who have passed an ID check and who both agree to join. Anyone watching a live can report it, and a report that someone looks under 18 is handled first.
  • In-app reporting. Every profile, chat and live room has a Report button. You can also block any member. Reports are reviewed by our team, and reports about children are handled first.
  • Action. We remove offending content, ban the account, and keep the information needed to prevent a return.
  • Reporting to authorities. We report CSAM and credible child-endangerment to the relevant authorities, such as national law enforcement and the NCMEC CyberTipline where applicable, and cooperate with investigations as the law requires.
  • Compliance. We follow the child-safety laws that apply to the places where the app is available.

If you see something

Use the in-app Report button, or email us at the address above with the profile name and what you saw. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
