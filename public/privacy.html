Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Heart Link ("we", "us") is operated by Empyrean App (Akhigbe Allen), and is a dating and live-video app for adults aged 18 and over. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Contact: heartlink43@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account and profile: email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication), name, age, gender, occupation, what you are looking for, bio, interests.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes you upload to your profile, chats or live rooms.
  • Messages you send, and call and live-room activity (who joined, gifts sent).
  • Location (optional): your approximate or precise location if you tap "Use my current location", or the country, state, city and neighborhood you type. It is used to show distance and people nearby.
  • Optional selfie verification: a selfie you submit to get a verified badge.
  • 18+ verification (needed to host or join private rooms): your full name, date of birth, ID type and number, a photo of your ID and a selfie. They are stored privately and reviewed by our team. We keep the last four digits of the ID number and a scrambled fingerprint to catch one ID used on several accounts.
  • Live video checks: while you are live or on stage, still pictures from your camera are sent every few seconds to an automated moderation service to detect nudity. They are checked in memory and not stored. We keep only the result (a score, the time and the room) when it leads to a warning, strike or report. We also keep strike and appeal records so restrictions can be applied and reviewed.
  • Matchmaking (optional): your religion, what you are looking for, partner preferences, your phone number, and your matches and chat messages. Your phone number is shown to a match only after you and that match both agree. If you show interest in a profile posted by our matchmaker, we pass your name, age, city and phone number to our matchmaker so they can contact you. People listed by our matchmaker have agreed to it, and we keep their details private. The one-time fee is paid on our website through Flutterwave; we receive the payment confirmation, not your card details.
  • Purchases: tokens are bought through Google Play. We receive the purchase confirmation, not your card details.
  • Device and usage data: a random device identifier used to detect fake and duplicate accounts, push-notification tokens, and basic technical logs.

How we use it

  • To run the app: create your account, show your profile to other members, match you, deliver messages and calls, and send notifications.
  • To keep members safe: review reports, check live video for nudity, confirm that people in private rooms are adults, block abusive accounts, and prevent fraud and fake accounts.
  • To process purchases and keep your token balance correct.
  • To show advertising and measure how often ads are viewed or tapped.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal data. Heart Link shows advertising, which helps us keep the app running. This includes sponsored adverts (images or short videos marked "Sponsored") that we place ourselves, and may include ads from third-party advertising networks such as Google AdMob. Advertising partners may collect your device or advertising identifier, approximate location and ad interactions (views and taps) to show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires, we ask for your consent before personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). We do not give advertisers your name, email, photos or messages.

Who we share it with

  • Other members see your profile, photos, videos, distance, and online status. Messages go to the person you send them to.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, notifications), an automated image-moderation service (Sightengine or Google Cloud Vision) for live video checks, Cloudinary (photo, video and voice storage), Flutterwave (website payments for Matchmaking), Agora (voice and video calls and live rooms), Google Play (billing), advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) for ads, and our hosting provider.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm (including child safety reports).

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete my data), which permanently removes your profile, photos, matches and chats. You can also request deletion without opening the app: see how to delete your data. We may keep limited records longer only where the law requires (for example purchase records) or to prevent a banned person from returning.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to it is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, turn off location by clearing it in your profile, block and report members, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct or export your data: email us and we will respond.

Children

Heart Link is only for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and delete such accounts when we find them. See our Child Safety Standards.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
